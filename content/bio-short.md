# Short Bio

wizardfromearth is a genre-resistant musician whose work moves freely through indie, ambient, experimental, rock, and rap. Beginning his creative journey in Providence and now based in Brevard County near Palm Bay, Florida, he has spent well over a decade building music entirely on his own terms.

A vocalist, rapper, guitarist, bassist, and keyboardist, wizardfromearth creates without allegiance to any single sound or musical influence. His universe draws inspiration from the strange futurism of *Futurama*, the mythology of *The Lord of the Rings*, and a perspective that refuses to be constrained by this world. Fluent in both English and German, he makes music for wherever the signal reaches next.
